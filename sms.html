Scriblet SMS

Scriblet provides conversational SMS messaging for organizations using Scriblet to collect, organize, and present information.

Each organization using Scriblet receives a dedicated SMS telephone number. An organization administrator provides that number to authorized contributors.

Authorized contributors initiate SMS communication by sending a message to their organization's Scriblet telephone number.

When a contributor initiates an SMS conversation, Scriblet may respond with confirmations, clarification requests, summaries, and other messages related to the contributor's organization's data collection and reporting activities.

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Scriblet does not sell or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in information with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.
