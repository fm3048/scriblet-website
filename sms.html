Scriblet SMS Messaging

Communicate naturally. Let Scriblet do the organizing.

Scriblet helps organizations collect information from people through natural conversations. When an organization uses Scriblet's SMS service, it receives a dedicated SMS telephone number that people can use to send information by text message.

You don't need to use special commands or follow a specific format. Simply send your message in your own words.

Scriblet interprets the message, organizes the information, and responds with a confirmation, clarification question, or other information relevant to your message.

How Scriblet SMS works

1. Your organization receives a dedicated number

Each organization using Scriblet receives its own dedicated SMS telephone number.

The organization's administrator provides that number to people who need to communicate information to the organization.

2. You start the conversation

Send a text message to your organization's Scriblet number.

Your message starts the SMS conversation. You can simply tell Scriblet what you need to communicate in your own words.

For example:

"The opening hymn is Great Is Thy Faithfulness, number 100."

or:

"The board meeting is Thursday at 7 PM in the fellowship hall."

3. Scriblet responds

Scriblet processes your message and responds with information relevant to what you sent.

Depending on the message, Scriblet may:

  • Confirm what it understood
  • Ask for clarification
  • Record information for the organization
  • Provide information related to your request

Scriblet does not require you to use specific keywords or a rigid message format.

SMS consent

By initiating an SMS conversation with Scriblet by sending a message to an organization's dedicated Scriblet number, you are initiating the conversation and consenting to receive SMS responses related to your message and the organization's use of Scriblet.

Message frequency may vary. Standard message and data rates may apply.

You can reply STOP at any time to opt out of further SMS messages.

Reply HELP for assistance.

Your SMS opt-in information and consent will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes. We may share information with service providers that help us provide SMS messaging and related services.

After you opt out

If you reply STOP, Scriblet will stop normal SMS messaging to your number.

To resume SMS communication, reply START.
