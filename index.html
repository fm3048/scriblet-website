Scriblet

Collect. Organize. Present.

inbox

Stop chasing information. Let Scriblet collect it for you.

Scriblet makes it simple.

Contributors communicate the way they already do — by text message or email, using their own words. Scriblet understands what they mean, organizes the information, and confirms what it understood.

When something isn't clear, Scriblet asks.

When everything is right, Scriblet has it ready to present.

Less coordination. Less chasing. Less copying and pasting. More time for what matters.

chat_bubble

You don't need to learn new software.

Tell Scriblet what you need. Then let everyone respond naturally.

personContributor"The board meeting is next Wednesday at 7 pm."
auto_awesomeScriblet"Got it. I have the Board Meeting on Wednesday, September 9, 2026 at 7:00 pm."

If something is missing or unclear, Scriblet asks for clarification.

personContributor"Jack will be covering for Bob this week."
auto_awesomeScriblet"What is Jack covering for Bob this week?"
personContributor"Jack will be greeter"
auto_awesomeScriblet"Got it. Jack will be greeter on Saturday, September 12, 2026, instead of Bob."

Scriblet doesn't guess. It asks.

No forms. No complicated interfaces. No training your volunteers to use another system.

Just communicate naturally.

route

From scattered messages to organized information

  1. 1
    sms

    Collect

    Contributors send information naturally, at their convenience.

  2. 2
    psychology

    Understand

    Scriblet interprets natural language and identifies the information in each message.

    It doesn't require contributors to use specific words or follow a rigid format.

  3. 3
    fact_check

    Confirm

    Before information becomes part of your final content, Scriblet can tell the contributor what it understood.

    If it's wrong, they can correct it.

    If something doesn't make sense, Scriblet asks questions until the information is clear.

  4. 4
    folder_open

    Organize

    Behind the scenes, Scriblet puts the information into the structure you've defined.

    You decide what information belongs where.

  5. 5
    campaign

    Present

    When everything is ready, Scriblet produces your finished content in the format you choose.

    Print it. Publish it. Share it digitally.

    Your information stays organized while your presentation stays consistent.

tune

Your format. Your content. Your workflow.

Scriblet doesn't decide what your bulletin, program, or report should look like.

You do.

Define the sections, information, order, and presentation that work for you.

Then use that structure whenever you need it.

Whether your final product is a printed bulletin, a digital publication, a web page, or another format, the underlying information remains organized and ready to use.

lightbulb

And this is just the beginning.

Scriblet was designed around a simple idea:

People shouldn't have to learn a system just to give you information.

They should be able to say what they need to say.

Scriblet turns those natural-language conversations into structured information that software can understand, organize, and present.

Wherever organizations collect information from multiple people and turn it into a consistent final product, Scriblet can help.
